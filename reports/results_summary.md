## 4. Propensity (redemption) model, campaign 18 targeted households

`past_redemptions_count` (the baseline ranking) is heavily tied -- 840 of 1,129 households have a value of exactly 0 -- so ties are broken with a small random jitter, fixed seed (`RANDOM_STATE`), before ranking.

|                             |   roc_auc |   pr_auc |   top_decile_lift |   pct_redeemers_captured_at_20pct_contact |   pct_redeemers_captured_at_40pct_contact |
|:----------------------------|----------:|---------:|------------------:|------------------------------------------:|------------------------------------------:|
| Calibrated XGBoost          |    0.8441 |   0.629  |            3.7213 |                                    0.6075 |                                    0.8178 |
| Baseline (past redemptions) |    0.7804 |   0.5633 |            3.7213 |                                    0.5794 |                                    0.729  |

- Both models tie on top-decile lift (3.72x) and capture nearly the same share of redeemers through ~20% contact (60.8% XGBoost vs. 57.9% baseline).
- XGBoost pulls ahead beyond that: at 40% contact it captures **81.8%** of redeemers vs. **72.9%** for the baseline, and wins outright on ROC-AUC (0.844 vs. 0.780) and PR-AUC (0.629 vs. 0.563).
